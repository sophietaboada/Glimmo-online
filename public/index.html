    How to play

    Each card has four features: number (1, 2, 3), shape (bolt, moon, star), color (blue, orange, teal) and shading (solid, striped, open).

    A match is three cards where each feature is either all the same or all different across the three cards.

    Tap three cards to claim a match. If there is no match on the table, three extra cards are dealt automatically. The game ends when the deck is empty and no matches remain.

    A match scores 1 point. A full spark, where all four features are different, scores 2.

    Group game: when you spot a match, tap MATCH!. If you were first, you have 10 seconds to pick the three cards. Right: +1 point, or +2 for a full spark. Wrong or out of time: −1 point, and you sit out until someone finds a match.
