    How to play

    Each card has four features: number (1, 2, 3), shape (diamond, squiggle, oval), color (red, green, purple) and shading (solid, striped, open).

    A set is three cards where each feature is either all the same or all different across the three cards.

    Tap three cards to claim a set. If there is no set on the table, three extra cards are dealt automatically. The game ends when the deck is empty and no sets remain.

    Group game: when you spot a set, tap SET!. If you were first, you have 10 seconds to pick the three cards. Right: +1 point. Wrong or out of time: −1 point, and you sit out until someone finds a set.
